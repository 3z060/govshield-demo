The problem

You cannot defend what you cannot see.

Public sector and enterprise estates sprawl across domains, subdomains, web apps, and APIs. Shadow assets appear, certificates lapse, and new CVEs land daily. Point-in-time audits go stale the moment they finish, leaving defenders reacting to yesterday's picture.

  • Unknown internet-facing assets and forgotten subdomains
  • Vulnerability backlogs with no clear prioritization
  • Threat intelligence disconnected from your real exposure
The solution

One continuous loop for exposure management.

GovShield discovers your external attack surface, assesses it against known vulnerabilities and misconfigurations, prioritizes what matters using severity and real-world exploitation signals, guides remediation, and verifies the fix — continuously.

  • Continuous discovery of the assets you own
  • Risk-based prioritization, not raw scanner noise
  • Verification that a fix actually closed the gap

Security principles

Authorization-first, always.

GovShield only assesses assets you own or are explicitly authorized to test. Scope is verified before any assessment runs. We never scan third parties without permission, and we never imply that any system is perfectly secure — security is a continuous practice, not a guarantee.
